# R.I.C.H.O. AI Operations Pilot — Client Onboarding

**Product:** R.I.C.H.O. AI Operations Pilot — Workflow Assessment & Action Plan  
**SKU:** RICHO-PILOT-199  
**Delivery type:** Digital service with human review  
**Scope:** One focused business workflow

## Purpose

This onboarding pack collects the minimum evidence needed to assess one real workflow, identify automation and AI opportunities, define controls, and produce a prioritised action plan. It is not a promise of production deployment or a guarantee of business outcomes.

## 1. Organisation context

Provide:

- Organisation or business name.
- Primary contact and role.
- Industry or operating context.
- Country or jurisdictions materially relevant to the workflow.
- The business outcome you want this workflow to support.

## 2. Workflow to assess

Choose **one** workflow. Describe it from trigger to completion.

- Workflow name.
- What starts the workflow?
- What is the desired completed state?
- Who owns the workflow?
- Who performs each major step?
- Which systems, applications, spreadsheets, inboxes, forms or data sources are used?
- Which handoffs occur between people or systems?
- Which approvals are required?
- Which steps are repetitive, slow, error-prone or difficult to audit?

## 3. Inputs and outputs

For each major input, state:

- Source.
- Format.
- Sensitivity or confidentiality.
- Expected quality.
- Common defects.

For each major output, state:

- Recipient or downstream system.
- Required format.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Required approval or review.

## 4. Current pain points

Rank the most important problems using **High / Medium / Low** impact.

Examples:

- Manual data entry.
- Duplicate work.
- Slow approvals.
- Missing information.
- Inconsistent decisions.
- Rework.
- Poor evidence trails.
- Difficult reporting.
- Customer delays.
- Excessive software switching.

For each problem, explain what happens today and how you know it is a problem.

## 5. Current evidence

Supply only information you are authorised to share. Useful evidence can include:

- Blank templates or forms.
- Redacted screenshots.
- Process notes.
- Existing SOPs.
- Example reports.
- Example input/output structures.
- Error categories.
- Approximate volumes and processing frequency.
- Typical processing time.

Do **not** provide passwords, API keys, private tokens, unrestricted customer databases, payment credentials or other raw secrets.

## 6. Constraints

Identify relevant constraints:

- Legal or regulatory obligations.
- Privacy requirements.
- Security requirements.
- Data residency requirements.
- Existing software that must remain.
- Systems that cannot be integrated.
- Budget or time constraints.
- Human approvals that must remain mandatory.
- Actions AI must never perform autonomously.

## 7. Success criteria

Define what a useful pilot result would mean. Prefer measurable criteria such as:

- Reduce manual steps from X to Y.
- Reduce average handling time.
- Improve completeness or consistency.
- Add an auditable decision trail.
- Reduce duplicate entry.
- Increase percentage of cases processed without rework.
- Identify a safe MVP worth implementing.

## 8. Human-review requirements

R.I.C.H.O. uses human-gated control for consequential decisions. Identify which outputs require human review before they can be:

- Sent externally.
- Published.
- Used for a payment or refund.
- Used to form a contract or legal commitment.
- Used to change permissions or security settings.
- Used to delete or overwrite data.
- Used to trigger a production deployment.

## 9. Pilot deliverables

The completed assessment is intended to produce:

1. **Current-state workflow map** — trigger, steps, owners, systems, handoffs and dependencies.
2. **Bottleneck and failure-mode analysis** — where delay, error, duplication or evidence loss occurs.
3. **AI and automation opportunity map** — candidate improvements, including cases where conventional automation is preferable to AI.
4. **Control design** — human review, approvals, validation, data boundaries and fail-closed conditions.
5. **QA and test plan** — what must be tested before any proposed solution could be trusted.
6. **Prioritised implementation roadmap** — smallest useful next changes, dependencies and recommended sequence.

## 10. Submission checklist

Before submitting your onboarding information, confirm:

- [ ] One workflow only has been selected.
- [ ] The workflow trigger and completion state are clear.
- [ ] Major systems and owners are identified.
- [ ] Pain points are prioritised.
- [ ] Success criteria are defined.
- [ ] Sensitive evidence is redacted where appropriate.
- [ ] No passwords, private keys or raw credentials are included.
- [ ] Mandatory human approvals are identified.
- [ ] You are authorised to provide the submitted information for this assessment.

## Scope boundary

This pilot is a structured workflow assessment and action plan. Production integrations, custom software development, enterprise deployment, ongoing managed services, additional workflows and third-party platform fees are separate scopes unless expressly included in a later written agreement.
